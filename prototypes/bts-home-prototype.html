Overview

BTS is a South Korean group of seven: RM, Jin, Suga, J-Hope, Jimin, V and Jungkook. They debuted on June 13, 2013 under Big Hit Entertainment, a small company at the time, with a sound rooted in hip hop and a habit of writing candidly about the pressures of being young. That directness, more than any single hit, is what pulled a global fandom together.

Across their catalog the group moved from the school-age frustration of their earliest records to the introspection of the Map of the Soul era, without losing the thread that started it all. This home page is the door into everything ARMY has documented here: the members, the discography, the eras, and the community around them.

2013 - 2014

The school trilogy

The earliest records read like a diary of being seventeen: school, expectations, and the itch to do something with it. O!RUL8,2?, Skool Luv Affair and DARK&WILD set the group's voice before anyone was watching closely.

O!RUL8,2? · Skool Luv Affair · DARK&WILD
2015 - 2016

The Most Beautiful Moment in Life · WINGS

The 화양연화 records turned toward youth as something fragile and fleeting, and it was the turn that made people pay attention. WINGS, in 2016, pushed the writing further and gave each member room of their own.

화양연화 pt.1 · 화양연화 pt.2 · WINGS
2017 - 2018

Love Yourself

A trilogy built around a simple, stubborn idea: that learning to like yourself is the whole work. It broadened the sound into pop, EDM and R&B, and it is the stretch where the group's reach became global.

LOVE YOURSELF 承 'Her' · LOVE YOURSELF 轉 'Tear'
2019 - 2020

Map of the Soul

Drawing on Jungian ideas of persona and shadow, this era looked inward just as everything around the group was getting louder. MAP OF THE SOUL : 7 gathered the threads of the whole journey into one record.

MAP OF THE SOUL : PERSONA · MAP OF THE SOUL : 7
2020

BE

Made largely at home during a year no one planned for, BE is quieter and more hand-made, the group writing about stillness and staying connected when touring stopped.
