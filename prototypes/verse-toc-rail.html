Jung Kook

indexable1 revision · updated Aug 9, 2026

Overview

Jeon Jung-kook, known mononymously as Jung Kook, is the youngest member of BTS. He debuted with the group in June 2013 at fifteen, and grew up quite literally on stage, from the group's earliest school-concept eras to global stadium tours.

Nicknamed the golden maknae by fans and members alike, he is known for covering an unusual amount of ground at once: main vocalist duties, dance line work, and a steady stream of covers and solo releases that chart on their own.

His place in BTS

Within the group he anchors the vocal line while sitting comfortably in the centre of most choreography. The dynamic between the youngest member and his six seniors has been a running thread of the group's variety content since the beginning.

Fans track his growth era by era: the wide-eyed rookie of 2 Cool 4 Skool, the breakout performer of the Love Yourself stadium years, the solo artist of the chapter that followed.

Solo work

His solo output spans official soundtrack singles and a full solo era, with releases that broke into global charts and streaming records. Each solo release becomes its own page as there is more sourced to say.

Beyond official releases, his song covers form a parallel discography of their own, widely circulated and often credited with introducing new listeners to the group.
