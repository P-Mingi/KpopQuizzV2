Jungkook전정국 · Jeon Jungkook

The youngest of BTS and the group's Main Vocalist and Center. He was fifteen when the group debuted in 2013 and grew up in public, becoming, over a decade, one of the most recognisable voices in pop.

Overview

Jeon Jungkook is the maknae of BTS, the youngest of the seven, and the member the group built many of its biggest choruses around. He auditioned as a young teenager, chose Big Hit over several other offers because he wanted to train alongside RM, and debuted at fifteen. That head start shaped him: he learned to sing, rap, dance and perform more or less simultaneously, and the fandom's nickname for the result, the "Golden Maknae", was only half a joke.

Within BTS he is the Main Vocalist and the Center, the member the formations tend to resolve around. Between the group's own releases he built an unusually open second life as a coverer of other artists' songs, an ongoing, unhurried vocal education that ARMY watched happen in real time.

His place in the group

Center is a specific job in a Korean group: it is the position the eye lands on first, and it usually falls to a member who can hold a stage without asking for it. Jungkook carries it quietly. As Main Vocalist his register sits at the emotional centre of a lot of BTS songs, the part that lifts a chorus into its final gear.

He grew up on stage, and you can hear the years of practice folded into how effortless he tries to make it look.

He is also, by a wide margin, the group's most restless performer, the one most likely to be found singing on a live stream at two in the morning. That appetite is the throughline from the fifteen-year-old trainee to the soloist he became.

Going solo

The GOLDEN era →

In 2023 Jungkook stepped out on his own without stepping away from the group. The single "Seven" arrived first that summer, followed by "3D", and in November by his solo debut album GOLDEN, led by "Standing Next to You". The record leaned fully into English-language pop and dance, a widening rather than a reinvention, and "Standing Next to You" in particular became one of the most-streamed songs by any K-pop soloist. It was the clearest statement yet of what Jungkook sounds like when the frame is only his.
