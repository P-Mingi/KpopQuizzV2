How points work

Fandom war points
Every finished quiz about a group adds 1 point to that group's fandom, whoever plays it, guests and replays included. The board counts the last 7 days and updates every hour; the change is against the 7 days before. General K-pop quizzes and blindtests do not count. Your main group, set in Settings, is the fandom pinned under the board.
Player points
Players are ranked by all-time XP, the XP that sets your level on your passport. Finishing a quiz for the first time earns 10 XP, 5 more at 70% or better and 15 more for a perfect score (replays earn none). Your daily streak, publishing quizzes and plays of your quizzes by other fans earn XP too.
Creator points
Creators are ranked by the plays their quizzes have received since they joined: every finished play counts, guests included. This week counts the plays of the quizzes they published in the last 7 days. Rising counts new followers in the last 7 days. Your pinned row is your all-time standing.
