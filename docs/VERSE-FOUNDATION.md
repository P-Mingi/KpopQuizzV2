fandom; our people must have enough freedom to reach that scale. Rethink
the navbar like fandom's. The base (page creation, page structure, pages
into pages, indentation) matters MORE than widgets; widgets are the
differentiator, not the foundation. Current look ruled "terrible, ugly":

## 2. How they reach 1,703 pages (the machine)

- THE PAGE IS CHEAP. Any title, one click, empty allowed. That is the
  whole secret. 64 active editors x 8 years did the rest.
- Three page MULTIPLIERS: (a) every audio track is a page, every variant
  too (Instrumental, remix, Japanese Ver.): ~35-45% of the wiki;
  (b) the /Gallery shadow tree: nearly every entity gets a media child
  page: ~20-25%; (c) fictional fan-out: each member exists as real bio +
  TinyTAN + BTS Universe + BT21 + game avatar + ship pages.
- CONVENTIONS AS ARCHITECTURE: slash subpages (X/Gallery,
  BTS/Discography/Gallery, SNS_Archive/2019), parenthetical
  disambiguation (Butter (album), Run BTS (song)), per-type section
  templates socially enforced (every member page = same H2 order:
  Early life > Career > Discography > Filmography > Credits > Awards >
  Trivia > Gallery > References > Navigation).
- THREE OVERLAID NAVIGATIONS: (1) curated navbar: max 4 custom tabs +
  EXPLORE, max 3 levels, max 10 children per node (platform caps: the
  proven shape); (2) faceted categories as the lateral web (a member
  sits in 1997 Births + Vocal Line + Maknae Line + Songwriters at once);
  (3) local links: hatnotes ("Main article:"), bottom navboxes,
  "To view the X gallery, click here" footer lines.
- PORTAL MAIN PAGE: zero prose, all image-links (member grid, cover
  grid, featured-of-the-month, dated timeline) + live freshness signals
  (1,703 PAGES counter, birthdays, Spotify playlist, Twitter feeds).
- Page anatomy that scales: infobox-right prose-left; grouped infobox
  sub-blocks (Personal / Professional / BTS Info / Soloist Info);
  computed fields (age 28, time in group 13 years); lead formula (bold
  name + hangul + one-sentence identity + date); tables only for
  homogeneous records; H2 = template sections, H3 = instances; TOC auto.

## 3. Their weaknesses (never copy)

Hand-duplication of every fact -> staleness divergence (group page
history stops in 2019 while member pages are current); trivia landfills
(60+ unsourced bullets); ad + chrome hell; maintenance categories
leaking to readers; galleries with zero media metadata; navigation
triplicated by hand and drifting; template discipline social, not
mechanical; anti-bot walls on CC-licensed content.

## 4. Honest gap analysis of OUR verse

What we already have that they cannot match: ONE relational source of
truth (7 members, 17 releases, 197 tracks for BTS, all sourced), so
cross-references and computed fields can never drift; SEO laws; the
block model (V-BUILDER-1) as a real document format; the covenant;
modern design capability; games as an acquisition machine.

What we are missing, and it is the CORE of a wiki:
1. CHEAP PAGE CREATION. Our pages are entity-generated (42 types) plus
   a constrained wiki tab. No free tree. This is gap number one.
2. PAGES INTO PAGES. No general parent/child hierarchy, no breadcrumbs
   on free pages, no move/rename, no subpage habit. (Infinite depth law
   exists on paper; the product does not deliver it.)
3. SPACE NAVIGATION. Our space shows a FLAT 12-tab bar (Home, Members,
   Discography, Timeline, Awards, Photocards, Collectibles, Wiki, Songs,
   Essays, Community, About): breaks our own lean-nav law and cannot
   carry 1,700 pages. Fandom's 4-tabs-3-levels menu is the proven shape.
4. THE LATERAL WEB. No tags/categories, no auto-navboxes, weak internal
   link density. Atlas (D4) is the seed, unbuilt.
5. MECHANICAL TEMPLATES. We can enforce per-type section order in the
   editor: they only manage it socially. Advantage wasted so far.
6. CREATION INVITATIONS. No ghost links (honest red links), no wanted
   pages, no stub states. Coverage % exists: good seed, alone.
7. READING SURFACE. Current space pages are card-soup: stacked cards,
   mixed rhythms, widget-first, weak typographic hierarchy. For a
   reference site the document page must be typographic: prose-left +
   facts-right, strict type scale, calculated grid, 66ch measure,
   restrained color. Verdict accepted: the owner is right, we
   over-invested in widget spectacle before the reading core.

Unfair advantage once built: we can SEED roughly 250-400 REAL pages for
BTS from the database on day one (members, releases, tracks, eras,
awards, tours), each sourced, each honest: fandom hand-built theirs over
8 years. Then the free tree lets humans do the rest. Honesty clause:
1,703 pages were written by PEOPLE. Architecture removes friction; the
community (recruitment, G-CLAIM) writes the pages. No fabricated pages,
ever: the covenant stands.

## 9. F0 - THE SKELETON CONTRACT (co-design 10)

The decisions that are expensive or impossible to reverse later. Each
carries a Cowork recommendation; owner rules each one.

C1 ONE PAGE MODEL FOR EVERYTHING. A single `pages` system per space:
   id, space_id, parent_id (the tree), slug, type, title, status
   (draft / published / trash), body = BLOCKS jsonb (the V-BUILDER-1
   format becomes the universal document format), optional entity
   binding (a member page binds to its idol row: facts auto-fill and
   never drift), created_by, timestamps. Companion tables:
   page_revisions (append-only history), page_links (the lateral web),
   redirects, tags + page_tags, nav_menus. Entity pages, free articles,
   galleries, indexes, and the space home itself are ALL rows of this
   one system. All SQL owner-gated at build time, as always.

C2 FLAT URLS + ETERNAL REDIRECTS. URL = /verse/bts/<slug>, FLAT. The
   tree (parent/child, breadcrumbs) is logical, stored in parent_id,
   NOT in the URL. Why: a page can move anywhere in the tree without
   its URL ever changing: zero SEO loss, zero broken links. Fandom
   works exactly like this (/wiki/Jung_Kook is flat; the tree is menu
   + categories). Rename or move = automatic permanent redirect kept
   FOREVER: a published URL never dies. Title collisions (song Butter
   vs album Butter) get the fandom convention: "Butter (album)".

C3 FULL REVISION HISTORY FROM DAY 1. Every save = one revision,
   append-only. Revert = a new revision (never destructive). Diff view.
   A "recent changes" feed per space (trust signal + moderation
   surface). Delete = TRASH with restore, never hard delete
   (fail-closed law extended to pages). Without history, community
   editing can never be opened safely; with it, moderation is cheap.

C4 EDITING LADDER. v1: curators + admin write, everyone reads. Guests
   and members do NOT edit yet. Community editing opens LATER, only
   after history (C3) + a moderation queue + G-CLAIM accounts exist.
   Honesty: fandom's 1,703 pages came from OPEN editing; we will open,
   but opening without history and moderation would be reckless.

C5 MECHANICAL INDEXABILITY. A stub (empty or near-empty page, no
   entity binding) = noindex + out of the sitemap, automatically,
   until it has substance (intro paragraph + one real section, or a
   data binding). No manual toggle to forget. Entity-seeded pages are
   indexable day 1: they carry sourced data. Extends min-gate +
   no-placeholder to the whole tree. Sitemap + BreadcrumbList JSON-LD
   generated from the tree (1000-row cap law respected).

C6 LINKS AS FIRST-CLASS OBJECTS. The editor gets a link picker
   ([[...]] behavior: search pages, insert link). Links are STORED
   relationally on save (page_links), which buys us: backlinks ("what
   links here"), auto-navboxes that never drift, orphan detection, and
   GHOST LINKS: a link to a not-yet-existing page renders visibly
   different (dashed) and opens "Create this page": fandom's red-link
   engine, the single biggest page-creation driver in wiki history,
   done honestly (a ghost link is never presented as content).
   Wanted-pages list = ghost links aggregated, sorted by demand.

C7 CONTROLLED TAGS. Tags are faceted (a page carries several: Vocal
   Line + 1997 Births + Songwriters) and CONTROLLED: creating a new
   tag is an explicit act, tags are listed and mergeable by curators.
   Auto-tags derive from data (birth year, era, release type). Each
   tag gets an auto-generated index page. Free-for-all tagging is
   refused: it rots into chaos (fandom's category sprawl).

C8 GALLERIES = COLLECTIONS, NOT SUBPAGES. A gallery page renders a
   QUERY over space_assets (the migration-146 rails: source, hash,
   moderation) + curator ordering. Unlike fandom's /Gallery subpages,
   every image keeps metadata and moderation. The /Gallery URL
   convention stays for readers; the storage is honest.

C9 SEARCH. Postgres full-text (title + text extracted from blocks),
   typeahead everywhere, and on no-hit the search offers
   "Create <name>" (creation invitation). No new dependency: pg FTS
   is fully sufficient at our scale. Search is the real navigation of
   a 1,700-page wiki.

C10 DESIGN SYSTEM SPEC (feeds the F5 prototypes). Base unit 4px;
   spacing scale in multiples; 12-col page grid; blocks snap to the
   grid; XxX micro-lattice (4x4 sub-cells) inside cells for stickers
   and fine placement (D1); fixed type scale; measure 66ch; density
   per the D6 70% ruling. TWO CANVASES ONLY: DOCUMENT (prose left,
   300px fact rail right with the grouped infobox, auto-TOC reading
   rail from H2/H3) and PORTAL (12-col, widgets live here). Minimal
   chrome, restrained color, fun in accents not structure.

C11 UNIFICATION, THE HONEST BIG ONE. The space home becomes a PORTAL
   PAGE inside the same pages system. The builder we just built
   (V-BUILDER-2/3: engine, overlay, style panel, content tab, image
   rail, members + hero editors) SURVIVES: its write target changes
   from verse_spaces.presentation to pages.blocks. Adaptation cost is
   real and assumed; nothing is thrown away. Saying otherwise would
   be dishonest: some rewiring weeks are the price of one clean model.

C12 SCOPE CUTS V1, honest. NO anonymous editing, NO realtime
   co-editing, NO wiki-markup dual mode (block editor only), NO
   per-page custom CSS, NO fictional-variant pages yet (TinyTAN, BTS
   Universe: the disambiguation architecture stays ready), NO
   interactive maps. Cut now, listed here, revisited only by owner
   directive.

C13 MIGRATION LAW: STRANGLER, NOT BIG-BANG. New tables built BESIDE
   the existing system; the ~250-400 BTS entity pages are SEEDED from
   the database; surfaces switch route by route, each switch with an
   SEO parity proof; Play stays byte-untouched (triple-proof). No
   flag-day rewrite, no dark weeks.
