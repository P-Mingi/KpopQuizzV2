## The rule

1. Source: the `genre` line of the infobox of the original work (the Header of `Infobox animanga` for a manga, light novel or original anime; the infobox of the web novel for Solo Leveling; the manhwa infobox for Tower of God and Omniscient Reader; the visual novel for Steins;Gate). The revision id read is stored with each series.
2. Each raw genre (the target of its link) has an ordered list of closed words in the table below, or none. The first word is its primary word.
3. The primary words, in the order of the infobox, without repeats, are kept up to 3.
4. If that leaves fewer than 3, the second words of the compound genres (Fantasy comedy gives Fantasy, then Comedy) are added in the order of the infobox until there are 3 or none left.
5. A raw genre with no word is dropped. Nothing is added that the infobox does not give; a series keeps as many words as its infobox yields (1 to 3).
6. The words are stored sorted in the order of the list. Compared as a set: Right = same set, Close = one word in common, Wrong = none.
