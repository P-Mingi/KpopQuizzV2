## The organic expansion tree (answer to "what pages to create")

This is exactly the fandom "pages into pages" model, grown from two
roots. From HOME and the MEMBER pages, the natural children:

FROM HOME (the ARMY / BTS portal):
- Members (7) [done as roots]
- Discography -> each RELEASE page (17) -> each release's TRACKS
- Eras / The story so far -> each ERA page (15)
- Awards -> the awards index (21 entries)
- Units / sub-units (Rap Line, Vocal Line, and real official units)
- ARMY (the fandom page itself: name origin, history)
- Company / label (Big Hit / HYBE) as a linked entity

FROM EACH MEMBER (e.g. Jungkook):
- Their SOLO releases + solo era
- The UNITS they belong to
- Their DISCOGRAPHY credits (writing/producing) as a section
- Galleries (deferred: C8 galleries-as-collections)
- Positions / role, linked to the other members holding it

The rule (fandom's real engine): a page grows children when a section
outgrows it. Discography section on HOME -> becomes 17 release pages.
A member's solo work -> becomes solo release pages. This is how one
space reaches hundreds of pages HONESTLY, one real entity at a time.

## P5 REFRAMED - the owner is right (auto-indexable quiz pages)

Owner rejected "accept partial indexing" and named the real problem:
if a new quiz is not indexed, nobody clicks it; only the big quizzes
get traffic; new interesting quizzes die from no visibility. This is
correct and it is a genuine product problem (UGC cold-start).

Honest engineering of the vision (I will not oversell it):
- We CANNOT declare a page "100% indexable" - Google decides. But we
  CAN make every quiz page MERIT indexing and be DISCOVERABLE, which
  raises the indexed fraction dramatically and fixes the real problem.
- SUBSTANCE without fabrication: wrap each quiz in REAL sourced
  context from our own data (the SAME entity/fact engine as the
  Verse): a real intro about the quiz topic, the relevant group/member
  facts (sourced, CC0), the questions themselves, related quizzes, the
  creator, difficulty, play stats. A "BTS discography quiz" page
  carries the real BTS discography as context - our data, not
  fabrication.
- DISCOVERY is the immediate win, before Google: every new quiz
  surfaces on its group hub, a "newest" feed, and related-quiz rails,
  so users find it regardless of indexing. This alone fixes "new
  quizzes get no views."
- HONEST CEILING: even substantial, 400+ structurally-similar pages
  will not ALL index; but far more will when they merit it, and
  internal discovery covers the tail.
- This is a DESIGNED workstream (PLAY-QUIZ-PAGES), prototype-first, on
  the Play/SEO track, parallel to the Verse. It reuses the Verse's
  entity-data engine to enrich quiz pages: the two sides share the
  moat (our structured, sourced K-pop data).

Sequencing for P5: design it prototype-first as its own workstream;
it can run on the SEO fork or a new Play slot. Not blocking the Verse
showcase. Immediate cheap slice already partly shipped (SEO-2 internal
links); the full "every quiz gets a substantial page + discovery
rails" is the next Play design.
