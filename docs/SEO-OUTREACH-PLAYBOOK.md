# kpopquiz.org Link-Building and Outreach Playbook

Goal: get kpop blogs, news sites, listicle authors, and creators to write about or
link to kpopquiz.org. Outreach wins when you make it easy and make them look good,
not when you ask for a favor. The copy/paste templates below are ready to send.

Note on copy: no em dashes or en dashes anywhere (project rule). Templates use
plain punctuation.

---

## 0. Prep before you send anything (1 to 2 days of work)

People only link to things that are easy to feature. Build these first:

1. A one-page media kit (a simple `/about` or a Notion/Google Doc) with: what the
   site is, that quizzes are free and made by fans, total quizzes, total plays,
   top groups covered, a logo, and 2 to 3 screenshots.
2. 3 to 5 "hero" quizzes with clean shareable URLs (your best BTS, BLACKPINK,
   aespa, Stray Kids quizzes). These are what you pitch.
3. One shareable stat. Pull a number from your own DB, for example "across N plays,
   only X percent of fans pass the hardest BTS quiz." Bloggers cite numbers.
4. (Optional, high value) an embeddable quiz widget (iframe). Every embed is a
   backlink. Even a "copy this link" button helps.

Without these, outreach reply rate stays low. With them, you have a reason for
them to say yes.

---

## 1. Build the target list (half a day)

Make a simple sheet with columns: Site/Creator, URL, Contact, Type, Angle,
Status, Date, Follow-up. Fill it from:

- Google searches (save the URLs):
  - `best kpop quiz` , `kpop quiz site` , `kpop trivia` , `how well do you know bts quiz`
  - `kpop quiz` + `intitle:listicle` style: `best kpop quizzes 2026`
  - `"kpop quiz" blog` , `kpop fan blog write for us` (guest-post targets)
- Site types to collect:
  - News/blogs: Soompi, allkpop, KProfiles, Koreaboo type sites, plus smaller fan
    blogs (the small ones reply far more often).
  - Listicles: any "best kpop quizzes / trivia sites" article already ranking. You
    want to be added to lists that already rank on page 1.
  - Creators: YouTube and TikTok accounts doing "kpop quiz challenge", "guess the
    song", "guess the idol" videos. They constantly need quiz material.
- Find the contact: look for a Contact page, an editor email, "write for us",
  bylined author email, or DM on Twitter/Instagram. Author-level beats a generic
  info@ inbox.

Target volume: 30 to 50 contacts to start. Reply rates are low, so volume matters.

## 2. Track A: blogs and news sites (pitch a free quiz to feature)

Best angle: offer ready-made content they can drop into a post, not a link
request. Tie it to a comeback or a calendar moment for urgency.

Subject: Free [GROUP] fan quiz for your readers (no signup)

```
Hi [Name],

I run kpopquiz.org, a free site where fans make and play K-pop quizzes. No signup,
no paywall.

With [GROUP]'s [comeback/anniversary/event] coming up, your readers might enjoy
this quiz: [URL]. It is fan-made, mobile-friendly, and takes about 2 minutes.

If it is a fit for a post or your socials, feel free to use it. Happy to make a
custom quiz for a specific article angle if useful. A link back to the quiz is all
I ask.

Either way, love your [recent article title]. Thanks for what you do for the
community.

[Your name]
kpopquiz.org
```

Why it works: low ask, timely, gives them content, compliments real work.

---

## 3. Track B: listicle link insertion (fastest wins)

Target articles that already rank for "best kpop quizzes" or "kpop trivia sites"
and do not list you yet. You are asking for one line to be added.

Subject: Quick addition for your "best kpop quizzes" post

```
Hi [Name],

Your post [article title] is the first thing I found searching for kpop quizzes,
nice list.

One you might add: kpopquiz.org. It is free, fan-made, and covers BTS, BLACKPINK,
Stray Kids, aespa, NewJeans and 30+ groups, with new quizzes added by the
community weekly. Direct link if helpful: [URL].

No worries if it does not fit. Thanks either way.

[Your name]
```

Why it works: editors update existing posts easily, and your angle (community,
always fresh) is a real differentiator vs static quiz pages.

Send 10 to 15 of these. This track usually returns the first dofollow links.

---

## 4. Track C: guest post (more effort, stronger links)

For fan blogs that accept contributions ("write for us" pages or smaller blogs).
Pitch a topic, not your site. The link to you lives naturally in the article.

Subject: Guest post idea: [specific topic]

```
Hi [Name],

I would love to write a guest piece for [blog]. A few ideas:

- "10 K-pop quiz questions only real ARMYs get right" (with answers)
- "How to host a K-pop trivia night with friends"
- "The hardest K-pop quiz questions, ranked"

I run kpopquiz.org so I can pull real questions and data, and I will write it to
your style and length. One natural link back to a relevant quiz is all I would
include.

Want me to send a full draft of any of these?

[Your name]
```

Why it works: you offer free, on-topic content with proof you can produce it. Keep
one contextual link, not five.

## 5. Track D: creators (YouTube and TikTok)

Creators want easy material for "quiz challenge" / "guess the song" videos. Offer
to do the work for them. DM or email.

```
Hey [Name], big fan of your [video type] videos.

I run kpopquiz.org (free K-pop quizzes). I can build a custom [GROUP] quiz for your
next video so you just hit play and react, no prep on your side. If you drop the
link in your description so viewers can try it, that is perfect.

Want me to set one up for [GROUP]? I can have it ready in a day.
```

Why it works: zero effort for them, content for you, a description link plus a
flood of brand searches when the video lands. Even nofollow YouTube links drive
real traffic and brand signal.

Bonus: give each creator a unique link or a custom quiz slug so you can see in GSC
and analytics which creator drove plays, and double down on those.

---

## 6. Sequencing, follow-up, and tracking

- Send in small batches (10 to 15 a day) so you can personalize each one. Generic
  blasts get ignored and can burn domains.
- One follow-up only, after 4 to 7 days, short: "Just floating this back up in case
  it is useful, totally fine if not." Then stop.
- Track every send in the sheet (Status: sent / replied / placed / no). Review
  weekly. Kill angles that get zero replies, scale the ones that land.
- Time the blog and creator pushes to comebacks and big releases. That is when fan
  sites publish and when "[group] quiz" searches spike.
- Measure success in GSC and analytics: new referring domains, referral traffic,
  and a rise in brand + "[group] quiz" impressions.

---

## 7. Do and do not

Do:
- Personalize the first line (reference a real, recent article or video).
- Lead with what you give them (content), not what you want (a link).
- Keep one relevant link per placement.
- Be a real member of the community (post, comment, help) before and between asks.

Do not:
- Mass-blast identical emails. Low yield, reputation risk.
- Buy links or use link farms. Google penalty risk, and it does not move the brand.
- Over-link or use exact-match anchor spam. Natural anchors ("this BTS quiz",
  "kpopquiz.org") are safer and look human.
- Spam subreddits or Discords with bare links. Add value first; links nofollow
  anyway, so the point there is traffic and goodwill.

---

## 8. Realistic expectations

- Listicle insertions (Track B): first dofollow links within 1 to 3 weeks.
- Blog features (Track A) and creators (Track D): steady once you tie them to
  comebacks.
- Guest posts (Track C): slower, a few per quarter, but the strongest links.
- Compounding effect: every editorial link and creator video lifts authority and
  brand searches, which is the exact signal kpopquiz.org needs to start ranking
  for "[group] quiz" terms (see SEO-AUDIT-2026-06-11.md, Sections 6 and 8).
