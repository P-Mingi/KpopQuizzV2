# G-PULSE - A LIVING COMMUNITY PAGE (future workstream · 2026-08-05)

Owner directive captured complete + deep research. Problem: the
community page has content (Daily Debate, Happening Now) but no
participation. Diagnosis: cold start + no payoff loop, NOT a missing
feature. Research: Wordle ritual, NN/g 90-9-1 ladder, Letterboxd,
Duolingo streaks, Mekler/Hanus gamification studies, Meta AI-profiles
and r/changemyview backlashes, Facebook engagement-bait demotion.

## Owner ideas, captured verbatim

1. XP for voting with a CLEAR ANIMATION.
2. A default vote from the kpophistory account taking a side with a
   small sentence. -> COVENANT COLLISION, see ruling below.
3. People can answer each other (replies on debates).
4. Launch NOTIFICATION announcing the new feature ("Agree or
   Disagree: take part in the community vote").
5. Profile section collecting ALL my opinions (votes + comments),
   PUBLIC ONLY IF I SAY SO.
6. Find more features for an alive community (research below).

## RULING NEEDED (idea 2, the honest version)

A house account that VOTES = fabricated activity (covenant breach;
receipts: Meta deleted its AI profiles Jan 2025 under backlash;
hidden AI bots in r/changemyview drew Reddit legal action). HONEST
REPLACEMENT, proven by Quora/Medium seeding: THE EDITOR'S TAKE:
every debate opens with a SIGNED, LABELED position from kpophistory
(badge "editorial", one-sentence argument), displayed as the opening
voice, NEVER counted in the percentages. People love dunking on the
house take: it seeds the room without a single fake vote.
OWNER: yes/no.

## The design (v1, all mechanics ranked by research fit)

1. VOTE-FIRST, TALK-SECOND. One tap agree/disagree; the % is HIDDEN
   until you vote: the reveal IS the payoff (NN/g lurker ladder).
2. THE CELEBRATION (owner's animation instinct, kept). Voting fires
   a satisfying animation (confetti burst + the bar racing to its %)
   + a small one-time daily XP. NO farmable per-action XP: the
   research is clear (Mekler, Hanus & Fox): paying for cheap clicks
   buys noise and kills intrinsic motivation. The juice is the
   reward; the XP is a daily ritual bonus, capped once/day.
3. REPLIES (owner idea 3): threaded replies on the debate, one level
   deep (reply to a take, not infinite nesting), existing moderation
   rails. Hide "0 comments" labels entirely until nonzero (empty-room
   law).
4. EDITOR'S TAKE opener (pending ruling above).
5. POST-GAME PROMPT: the score screen pipes into the debate: "8/10
   sur le quiz aespa. Hot take du jour: d'accord?" One tap votes
   right there. Highest-intent moment on the site.
6. OPINION BOOK (owner idea 5): profile section listing all my votes
   + takes, PRIVATE BY DEFAULT, per-item or global opt-in to public
   (Venmo's public-by-default backlash = the lesson). Public version
   becomes a taste-identity grid (Letterboxd model) on MY VERSE.
7. PREDICTION LEAGUE (new, from research): before a comeback: "guess
   the first-week streams / first win date"; scored on results day =
   an automatic return visit + a skill-based score on the profile.
   Real data only, resolves against sourced numbers.
8. DEBATE STREAK, gentle: voted N days running, with freezes, no
   guilt copy (Snapchat streak-anxiety findings).
9. SHARE CARD: spoiler-free "J'ai vote + le %" card (Wordle grid
   logic), never gated.
10. LAUNCH NOTIFICATION (owner idea 4): one-time in-app notification
    to existing accounts announcing the Daily Debate ("give your
    opinion"), honest copy, once, dismissible. Plus the post-game
    prompt as the evergreen discovery path (better than broadcast:
    Userpilot/Appcues data).

## Cold-start playbook (week 1)

Shrink the surface (one debate, one feed) · editor's take + 2-3 real
signed replies daily · game events wired into Happening Now (already
done, keep) with honest aggregates · post-game prompt live · hand-
recruit 5-10 opinionated superfans from the recruitment thread
(founding badge), answer every comment for two weeks.

## Anti-patterns (bound by covenant, with receipts)

No fake users/votes ever (Meta, r/changemyview) · no engagement bait
copy ("share if you agree": Facebook demotes it platform-wide) · no
notification spam (one launch ping, then contextual only) · no
visible emptiness ("0 comments" hidden) · no punitive streaks · no
public-by-default opinions (Venmo).
