Status: FINAL, approved by owner Jul 2026 after curation + web fact-check pass
(NewJeans no-official-leader corrected; Liz/Rei/Winter trait lines re-sourced).
Build-ready.

Axes: energy (quiet 0 -> loud 100), chaos (calm 0 -> feral 100), care (caretaker 0
-> cared-for 100), craft (stage 0 -> studio/creator 100), heart (soft 0 ->
tough-love 100), spotlight (center 0 -> lowkey 100).

Weights: each option lists axis:points (5 = strong, 3 = medium, 2 = light).

## The 10 questions (original, patterned on the classic formats)

1. Your role in the friend group?
   a. The one who plans everything - care:0, energy:3
   b. The chaos that makes it fun - chaos:5, energy:5
   c. The quiet one who roasts everyone once an hour - heart:5, energy:0(-anchor: quiet), spotlight:5
   d. The baby everyone protects (and underestimates) - care:5(cared-for), chaos:3

2. It is 2am on tour. Where are you?
   a. Asleep hours ago, self-care is discipline - chaos:0, care:3
   b. In the studio, one more take - craft:5, spotlight:5(lowkey)
   c. Feral hotel-hallway content with the members - chaos:5, energy:5
   d. Baking / gaming / quietly recharging alone - heart:0(soft), energy:0

3. Someone in the group is having a rough day. You:
   a. Notice first, talk to them one-on-one - care:0(caretaker), heart:0
   b. Make them laugh until they forget - energy:5, chaos:4
   c. Say nothing, sit next to them, share food - spotlight:5, heart:2
   d. Write them into your next lyric - craft:5

4. First impression people get of you?
   a. Intimidating, then it collapses in 5 minutes - heart:5-then-soft (heart:3, chaos:2)
   b. Sunshine, immediately - energy:5, heart:0
   c. Mysterious main-character energy - spotlight:0(center), energy:2
   d. Polite and quiet (they have no idea yet) - energy:0, chaos:2(hidden)

5. Pick a stage moment:
   a. The killing part everyone screenshots - spotlight:0, craft:0(stage)
   b. The ad-lib nobody expected - chaos:5, craft:2
   c. The perfectly clean formation you drilled - care:3, craft:0, chaos:0
   d. The self-produced track finally performed live - craft:5

6. Group project (school edition). You are:
   a. The leader who actually emails the teacher - care:0, energy:2
   b. The one who does it all at 3am and aces it - craft:4, chaos:3
   c. The morale officer contributing memes and one slide - chaos:5, energy:4
   d. The one who quietly fixes everyone else's slides - spotlight:5, care:0

7. Your comfort activity?
   a. Gym / physical, body busy = mind quiet - heart:4, craft:0
   b. Cooking or baking for people - heart:0, care:0
   c. Games / anime / one specific hyperfixation - energy:0, chaos:2
   d. Journaling, photos, making something - craft:5, spotlight:4

8. How do you argue?
   a. Calm words, devastating accuracy - heart:5, energy:0
   b. I do not argue, I go quiet (scarier) - spotlight:5, heart:3
   c. Loud for 90 seconds then buying you food - energy:5, heart:0
   d. I win by making you laugh mid-sentence - chaos:5

9. Pick a vibe (trust your first click):
   a. Gold hour, film camera, quiet street - spotlight:5, craft:3
   b. Neon, 1am, convenience store run - chaos:4, energy:4
   c. Home, blankets, someone else cooked - care:5(cared-for), heart:0
   d. Stage lights, ears ringing, alive - spotlight:0, energy:5

10. What do people thank you for most?
    a. Being the reason it got done - care:0, craft:2
    b. Being the reason it was fun - chaos:5, energy:4
    c. Being the one who listened - heart:0, spotlight:3
    d. Being unapologetically yourself first - spotlight:0, heart:2
