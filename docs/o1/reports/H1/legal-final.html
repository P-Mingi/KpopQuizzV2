Privacy

What FanQuiz and its sites do with your data, in plain English.

Who runs this

FanQuiz (fanquiz.org) is the home of one account shared by three fan quiz sites: KpopQuiz (kpopquiz.org), OtakuQuiz (otakuquiz.org) and FanQuiz (fanquiz.org). They are separate websites run by the same owner.

You make your account once and use it on any of the sites.

The data controller is:

Nuri, 6 rue Rémusat, 75016 Paris, France

What your account holds

You sign in on each site like this:

  • KpopQuiz: Google, Discord or an email link
  • OtakuQuiz: Google or an email link
  • FanQuiz: Google or an email link

We receive your email address and, when you use Google or Discord, your display name and a link to your avatar. We never receive your password for those services.

Your email address is kept by the sign-in system and is never shown to other fans.

What one account shares between the sites

Other sites see only what the block below lists. Nothing else about you moves from one site to another.

What your passport shares

Other sites can see

  • Your username
  • Your avatar
  • Your level, only if you make it public
  • The sites you joined, only if you choose to show them

Other sites never see

  • Your email address
  • How you sign in
  • Your private history

Your quiz history stays on the site where you played.

When you move from one site to another while signed in, your browser carries a ticket that lasts 10 minutes and names no one. The second site swaps a one time code, valid for 60 seconds, for a session. We log each move (the two sites, the result, a hashed IP address and your browser family) and delete the log after 90 days.

What each site keeps for itself

Each site keeps its own record of you, and it is not copied to the other sites:

  • Your points, level, streaks and play counts on that site
  • The quizzes you create there, with their plays and likes
  • Your answers and history on that site
  • The profile details you set on that site, such as bio, banner and favourite pick
  • When you joined and when you were last seen there, which only you can see

On KpopQuiz, your bio, banner and favourite groups belong to your account profile. The other sites do not show them.

Audience measurement

We count visits with Vercel Web Analytics. It sets no cookie and keeps no personal data about you.

It records anonymous events, such as a page being opened. We show no advertising on the sites.

Cookies and local storage

The sites use only what they need to run. We use no advertising cookies, no tracking cookies and no third party analytics cookies.

  • sb-<project ref>-auth-token (and its numbered parts): keeps you signed in. Each site sets its own, on its own domain.
  • __Host-pp_tx: set for at most 5 minutes while you move from one site to another, then cleared. Over plain http on a local test host it is called pp_tx.
  • nq_anon: a random guest id, so that a quiz you played before signing in can be added to your account.
  • kq_sl: only on KpopQuiz, remembers the share link you came from.
  • preferred_locale: only on KpopQuiz, your language choice.
  • verse_nav: only on KpopQuiz, your choice of navigation in the Verse.
  • world: only on KpopQuiz, your choice between Play and Verse. It has nothing to do with the other sites.
  • theme (local storage): light, dark or system. Each site keeps its own.
  • fq.net.caps.v1 (local storage): how often a link to another site was shown, and which ones you dismissed. It stays in your browser.
  • fq.net.via (session storage): for this tab only, the site link you came from. It is removed after you sign in.
  • anime.spoiler.v1 (local storage): only on OtakuQuiz, your spoiler setting for each series.

Services we use

We use these services to run the sites:

  • Supabase: the database and the sign-in, where your account and quiz data are stored
  • Vercel: hosting and audience measurement
  • Google: sign-in, if you choose it
  • Discord: sign-in on KpopQuiz only, if you choose it
  • Resend: sending the sign-in emails

Your data is hosted in the European Union: Supabase in Ireland and Vercel servers in Dublin.

Some of these providers are based in the United States. They are covered by the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission.

We do not sell your data.

Leaving a site and deleting your passport

You can leave OtakuQuiz. Its record of you is removed, and your account and your other sites stay. You cannot leave your last site, and you cannot leave KpopQuiz.

You can delete your whole passport from the settings of any site. On KpopQuiz, open Settings and choose "Delete account".

You type your @username to confirm, and you must have signed in within the last 10 minutes. You are signed out everywhere and we send you an email.

You then have 7 days to change your mind: sign in again and the deletion is cancelled. When the 7 days are over, everything is deleted.

The quizzes you published are kept under "deleted user" unless you ask to remove them too.

How long we keep your data

We keep your play history as long as your account exists.

You can ask for a copy of your data. Write to contact@fanquiz.org and we send you an export.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to correct it, to erase it, to restrict how we use it, to object to its use and to receive it in a portable form.

To use a right, write to contact@fanquiz.org. We answer within one month.

You can also complain to the CNIL, the French data protection authority.

Children

The sites are not for children under 15. If you are under 15, please do not create an account. If we learn that we hold data from a child under 15, we delete it.

Law and changes

French law applies to this policy.

We tell you about important changes with a banner and by email.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or a request about your data: contact@fanquiz.org.
