## Goal
The pixel reference for every new screen of otakuquiz.org, fanquiz.org and the passport, and the kpopquiz.org deltas,
validated by the owner before any visual merge of Wave 2.

`design/o1-prototype/` (never shipped in the app, never imported by app code). No build, no dev server of the app.

## Rule 18 (owner, 16 October 2026): the prototype is the kpop screens re-skinned
OtakuQuiz is kpopquiz.org with small changes, not a new design. Every anime frame starts from its kpop twin (the row of
`O1/docs/o1/briefs/TWINS-draft.md`, then F0's frozen `docs/o1/contracts/TWINS.md`) and keeps its layout, components,
interactions and copy structure. Build the kpop twin frame from the pinned kpop design reference
`O1/docs/design/growth-v12/prototype.html` (it holds every v11.2 page and the v12 views) with the real tokens of
`O1/apps/quiz/src/styles/ux-v1/a0.css`, then show the anime frame next to it, at the same width, with each difference
marked by its code (T, B, V, C, F, N, A1 to A7; Tn, Bf, H1 to H4 for FanQuiz). Allowed differences are only those of the
row; no new screen and no new block outside the listed anime blocks. FanQuiz frames use the same kit with the neutral
theme. Never open kpopquiz.org itself (production is read only by parity.sh). Frames you made before this rule are
reviewed by R against it; rebuild what departs from its twin.

CR-002 to CR-006 are refused for O1 (owner, 2026-10-18): no frame, no placeholder for the welcome card, "How I appear
here", "Keep anime in your passport", the post-result oshi row or the kpop welcome card.

D-029 (2026-10-18): the lanes O5 (Which character, Name them all, This or that), X1 (crossover tag, hub fact line) and
E1b (/admin/review) are moved after the run: no frame for them. Finish first what O1, O2, O3, O4, O6, P1 and H1 build.

     private-use notice), Opening of the day, /daily, /about, /editorial-policy, footer with the fan-made notice, 404,
     title card SVG samples, character tiles (monogram and role shape, never a character image), Team creator
     profile, /me, /u (W3), /settings (anime sections), sign-in sheet (W5 case B, without the passport button),
     welcome card (W5 cases A and C, never naming K-pop), "How I appear here", oshi row (star icon).
   - Hub (FanQuiz, neutral theme): home (network page), /privacy, /passport (one-world and two-world, W1), /u (W1b),
     /settings (W4, four tabs, the S6 delete steps, leave a world), sign-in page, optional /directory.
   - kpopquiz.org deltas, each shown as a before and after and marked "flag": footer line, Your sites menu block (W2),

3. `contrast.mjs` (node, no dependency) and `contrast.md`: every foreground and background pair the frames use, light

## Rules specific to D0
- No image of a character, poster, logo or cover; no real fan photos. Placeholders are geometric.
- Real data only: numbers in frames are marked as fixture values or "New"; never an invented count presented as real.
- External resources: the Google Fonts stylesheet for M PLUS Rounded 1c is allowed in the prototype; nothing else
  from outside (no CDN scripts, no remote images).
