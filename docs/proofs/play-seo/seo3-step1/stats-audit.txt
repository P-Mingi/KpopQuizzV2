Threshold-30 law (per memory: rankings-unlock-threshold)
========================================================
Stats that are leaderboard-ish (pass rate, avg score, fastest time) show
provisional "early results" tier when total_completions < 30, else the
full number. Play/completion counts always shown (they are counts, not
rankings).

Related-by-entity (U4) - shared-tag audit
=========================================
FINDING: no `tags` or `topic` column exists on quizzes. QuizSettings jsonb
holds only {timer, timer_seconds, shuffle, show_answers}. No era/album/
member field on quizzes.
CONSEQUENCE: "shared tags" refinement of related quizzes is NOT feasible
today. The existing same-group + play-count ordering IS the entity-based
signal (same-group is the strongest entity link we have).
IMPROVEMENT WE CAN DO: also include creator (same-creator variants
exist), and vary which orderings each quiz sees so related rails differ.
