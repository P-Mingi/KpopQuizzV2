B. Newest quizzes surface (U7 discovery lever)
==============================================
Modified: apps/quiz/src/app/[slug]/group-quiz-page.tsx (+30 lines)

Before: GroupFeed (client component) SSR'd only the 10 POPULAR quizzes.
Newest quizzes were reachable only after a user clicked the "Newest" tab
(client-side fetch). Googlebot ran the initial HTML which did NOT
contain the newest quizzes, only the popular ones.

After: an SSR strip "Newest {group} quizzes" renders 5 real created_at-
ordered quizzes into the initial HTML, next to the article-links
section. Filtered to items NOT already in the popular list, so the strip
adds signal instead of duplicating chrome.

Fetch: getQuizzesByGroup(group.id, 'newest', 0, 5) - the existing
'newest' sort tab that GroupFeed uses, just fetched server-side.

Why this matters: a brand-new quiz gets an inbound crawlable link from
its group hub the moment it publishes. Google recrawls the hub (which
is high-priority) and follows the newest link to the new /q/* page.
This is the honest "discovery before Google" lever the mission cites.

No fake data. The created_at date is real from the quiz row. Empty
strip (when no truly-new quiz exists) renders nothing.
