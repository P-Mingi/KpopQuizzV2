TINY tier (playCount <= 5, e.g. brand-new NewJeans quiz with 0-2 plays)
  intro variant A ("Be one of the first") or B ("2 players have tried it")
  QuizStatsBlock: renders NOTHING (playCount === 0 branch) OR
                   shows Plays: 2 only (extra stats mostly empty)
  QuizHallOfFame: empty state (0 entries)
  Related quizzes: 6 same-group (still populated because the group has
                   older quizzes)
  Article links: e.g. "How Well Do You Know NewJeans" +
                       "aespa vs NewJeans" + "All NewJeans quizzes"
  JSON-LD: single interactionStatistic (Play=0 or 2),
           hasPart with real questions, no CompleteAction if
           completions=0

WHAT IS UNIQUE PER QUIZ (across all 3 tiers)
============================================
  1. quiz.title           - always unique
  2. quiz.description     - templated but filled with group/difficulty/
                            creator/question count/plays/avg score
  3. quiz.questions[]     - the biggest per-quiz unique text pool
                            (rendered crawlably via U8)
  4. quiz.fun_facts[]     - unique color per question
  5. Real stats numbers   - literally unique per quiz
  6. hallOfFame entries   - different real players per quiz
  7. Related quiz slugs   - depends on the group's other quizzes
  8. JSON-LD hasPart      - the question array is unique per quiz
  9. Article links        - varies by group

WHAT IS SHARED (the chrome, acceptable)
=======================================
  1. Page shell layout, breadcrumbs
  2. Owner actions, hall-of-fame section header
  3. Stats block layout (values differ)
  4. Article-link labels (constant per group, differ across groups)
  5. Related-quizzes section header

That is EXACTLY the pattern the anti-thin bar wants: the chrome is
shared, the FILLED content is per-quiz real data.
