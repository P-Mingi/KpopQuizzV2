Why these three pages read genuinely differently
===========================================================

The chrome is the same for all three (breadcrumb, player, hall of
fame, related list, article links, JSON-LD wrapper). Everything else
is per-quiz real data:

  - Intro tail:  three DIFFERENT sentence structures fire
                 (branch D, branch C, branch B). Different lengths,
                 different phrasings, different embedded numbers.
  - Stats cells: BIG has 7 cells with the ranking-ish trio visible;
                 MID has 7 cells with the same trio; TINY has 3 cells
                 and a gate line (no trio at all). The rendered HTML
                 is materially different for TINY vs the other two.
  - Numbers:     every value (2060/853/5, 53/57/-, 224/62/2, etc.) is
                 unique to the quiz.
  - JSON-LD:     BIG and MID emit Complete+Like counters; TINY emits
                 Complete only (likes=0, so no Like counter);
                 numberOfQuestions differs (8/20/10);
                 hasPart varies in length and content per quiz.

No two of these pages share more than the template shell. That is the
anti-thin bar from docs/PLAY-QUIZ-PAGES.md line 79, satisfied without
the O1/O3 trivia work (which stays deferred to Cowork's migration 149).
