# KpopQuiz Content Plan: 20 Original Articles (Track B)

These are original, indexable articles (not the noindex /news aggregator). Each
ranks in Google and is built to get cited by AI engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity,
Gemini) for K-pop quiz queries. Every article funnels to a real quiz, blind test,
or game.

No em dashes anywhere (titles, copy, meta, schema). Plain hyphens / colons.

## Where they live
A new indexable articles section: `/articles/[slug]` (or `/guides/[slug]`),
in the sitemap, footer-linked, with `Article` JSON-LD, a visible author/byline
("KpopQuiz Team"), a "Last updated: [date]" line, breadcrumbs, and a beautiful
on-brand template (pink #e8457a, rabbit mascot, dark-mode aware) built with the
ui-ux-pro-max + frontend-design skills.

## AI-citation rules baked into every article
- Lead each section with a direct 40-60 word answer (extractable).
- H2/H3 phrased like real search queries.
- Include real numbers from your DB (cited, dated) where relevant.
- Comparison articles use a real table. Listicles use numbered lists.
- A short FAQ + FAQPage schema at the bottom.
- 2-4 internal links to quizzes/blind tests/games per article.
- Write for humans, never chunked AI-bait, never fabricated stats.

## Content type mix (by AI citation share)
Comparisons ~33%, definitive guides ~15%, original data ~12%, listicles ~10%.
The list below is weighted toward those.
