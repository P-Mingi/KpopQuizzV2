## 5. Decisions and deviations

1. SEO lock beats the prototype copy: the H1 is today's `Name that<br>K-pop song` (no period), the lead is
   today's intro, the FAQ is today's 7 questions under "Frequently asked questions" (prototype: 4 new ones).
2. Today's hub linked 18 theme playlists; the prototype has no place for them, so a "Theme playlists" link row
   closes the Play by group section (else those pages lose their only internal link).
3. BreadcrumbList JSON-LD kept (today's), without a visible crumb (the prototype has none).
4. Mixes offered = the ones the engine serves; "Recent hits", "Legends", "Speed round" are not offered (section 6).
   By generation lists 1st to 5th (all have songs).
5. The board shows total answer time (the real tie-break); the daily stores score + time, no points.
   "Resets at midnight UTC": the daily rotates on the UTC date.
6. Free play: third stat = Fastest answer (no XP is awarded today); daily signed in = the real XP.
7. Question copy = the prototype's ("Which song is this?", "Who sings this?").
8. Auto-next 3 s (spec) instead of the live 2.6 s.
9. Dark results stats box: `rgba(0,0,0,.18)` (the prototype's dark capture shows the light box, a
   `[data-theme]`-only rule, with unreadable labels; 16.9 AA wins).
10. Song-row play buttons are 44 px on coarse pointers (16.9), 40 in the prototype.
11. "Challenges waiting" is not shown: nothing says who a challenge is for (section 6).
12. Challenge links are `/blindtest?c=<code>` (the hub opens them), since `/bt/c/[code]` is outside P6 paths.
