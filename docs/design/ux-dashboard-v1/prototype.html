How ranked works

The run

10 songs drawn from the whole pool by the server: 4 easy, 4 medium, 2 hard by community accuracy, 6 song rounds and 4 artist rounds, 10-second clips, no skip, no group choice. Same rules for everyone.

Points per song

100 for a correct answer, up to +100 for speed (full bonus under 2 seconds, down to 0 at 10), multiplied by your combo: x1.0, then +0.1 per song in a row, capped at x2.0. One miss resets the combo. A perfect fast run is about 2,900.

Season score

The sum of your 5 best runs this season. A new run only counts if it beats your 5th best. Your score never goes down. Ties break on average answer speed.

Tiers

Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond, Master, each in three divisions (III, II, I). Legend is the top 100 Masters, recomputed every night. Promotions are instant, there are no demotions inside a season.

Seasons

8 weeks. At the reset you keep your rank title and XP, your season score restarts, and your first 5 runs are placement runs shown as "3 / 5 placed". Season badge on the passport, pinnable.

Fair play

The song set and clip timings are generated server-side, answer times are measured from the server clip start, 15 runs per day, a quit run is recorded with the songs answered so far.
