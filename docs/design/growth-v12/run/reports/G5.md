### Which member are you

- Eight questions: the first eight active rows by `ord` (the table has ten; owner decision 2). Picture-free, four big
  answer cards, no timer, Back, a progress bar.
- Result: the member whose stored axis profile is nearest to the player's point. The card shows the name, the
  member's **public role** (`roles.ts`: leader, vocal / rap / dance position, production credit, unit, oldest or
  youngest; "Member of <group>" when none is listed), two sentences and three traits **about the player**, built
  from the three strongest axes of the profile. An initial, never a photo.
- The stored `trait_lines` are **not shown**: many state private habits of real people ("Bakes brownies at 2am",
  "Cats over people, usually", "Leader since age 18, tired since age 19"). The old floored match percentage
  (minimum 55) is gone too: an invented number.
- "N% of <fandom> got <member>" and "How everyone came out" (A1's `Distribution`) from the real counts, only when
  the group has at least 20 saved results; otherwise both are absent. The header prints the real total.
- Share my result (A1's share sheet, canonical URL, story card), plus "Save as an image": Story 1080 x 1920 and
  Square 1080 x 1080 (A1's `storyCardFile`, gradient ground, no picture).
- "Make <member> your bias tag?": signed out opens the sign-in sheet and resumes after sign-in
  (pending action `g5-bias`); signed in posts `{ bias: <member> }` to `/api/auth/update-profile`.
- Retake returns to the intro. "Also on <group>" (blindtest with its real song count, the group's quizzes) under the
  intro, only when the group's blindtest is playable.

### Scoring rule (owner decision 1)

The removed engine summed the weights and normalised them. A 0 weight is a real anchor in the approved bank
("care:0" is the caretaker end) and a sum drops it. Measured over every possible answer sheet of the eight
questions (`run/reports/G5/checks/reachability.txt`): with the old rule Changbin, Han, Dahyun and Beomgyu can
never be a result and one member takes more than half of the sheets in 5 groups (Jimin 52%, Karina 66%). G5 reads each weight as a
position on its axis (weight / 5 x 100) and takes the mean per axis, an untouched axis staying at 50: all 97
members are reachable, the largest share in any group is 57% (Jisoo, 4 members) and in Stray Kids 21%.

## 4. Data safety

- No production write from any check. Every page of the spec is wrapped with `guardWrites`; the two POSTs are
  answered locally. The only direct POSTs of the spec carry `{ quiz: 'nope' }`, refused with 400 (flag on) or 404
  (flag off) before any read.
- The route recomputes the result from the answer sheet on the server. A signed-in fan writes through the session
  client (existing RLS policy, own `user_id` only; the existing unique index keeps one row per fan, group and UTC
  day). A guest row is inserted with the service role and no `user_id`, at most once per device, quiz and day
  (localStorage), with an in-memory limit of 12 a minute per caller. These are the rules of the removed route.
- A bridge result is stored in `personality_results` with `group_id` = the group the player got and
  `member_name = '@kpop-demon-hunters-quiz'`, so it never mixes with member results (the member distribution only
  counts the group's own profile names). Owner decision 4.
- Fail soft: the page never waits for the save; a failed read hides the numbers or gives a 404.
- No secret printed or written. No new dependency. No SQL file.
