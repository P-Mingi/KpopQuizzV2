How it works
- The member list is read from `idols` (active, not detached, in `ord` order) with the cookie-free public client,
  cached like the other catalogue reads. `spellings.ts` never decides who is in a group: it only adds what a fan
  may type for a member (other romanizations, the birth name alone and with the family name, the Hangul of the
  stage and birth names). A Hangul value stored in `idols.name_hangul` is accepted too; kana, Chinese and Thai in
  that column are ignored.
- Matching: everything is compared after normalising (case, spaces, dots, hyphens, apostrophes, accents; Hangul
  typed as jamo is composed). An exact spelling always wins. A one-letter typo (one letter changed, missing or
  added) is accepted only against spellings of 5 letters or more and only when it points at one member.
- A round: 60 seconds for every group, the clock is derived from the start time (a throttled tab gains no time),
  Enter submits, a found slot reveals its name, "Give up" reveals the rest, naming everyone ends the round at once.
- Result: found / total, the time, "you gave up" when it applies, Share (the v11 share sheet with a typographic
  story image, no photo), Again.
- The write: one `POST /api/name-all/result` per finished round, sent once, never awaited by the screen. The
  server checks the names against the database roster, makes the round id itself, and stores one row per member,
  as the table has always held them. The browser id only keys a burst limit (12 rounds a minute); it is not stored.
- Community lines on the result, both positive, both hidden until they exist: "N% of rounds named all 8" and
  "Named first most often: <member>". They need the pending SQL and 30 finished v12 rounds of the group. A tie
  for "named first" prints nobody. Nothing says who is named least: the published shape has three fields
  (`rounds`, `perfectPct`, `namedFirst`) and a unit test pins it.

## 10. Owner decisions (none blocks)

1. **NCT.** The database has 9 active members under `nct`, the NCT 127 line-up. Built: no page. Options: keep it
   out, or add an `nct-127` group to the data and list it.
2. **Old rounds.** The 7,524 existing rows come from the older game (other timers, no order). Built: they are
   kept and ignored by the new stats, which start from zero. The alternative (counting them in "named all") would
   mix 30 to 180 second rounds with 60 second ones.
3. **Before the SQL is applied**, a finished round is still stored, in the four old columns. Those rows also feed
   the Verse recognition figure, as the older game's rows did. If the owner prefers nothing stored until the SQL
   is applied, it is one branch to remove in `saveRound`.
4. **Stats threshold.** A community line appears from 30 finished rounds of a group (`STATS_MIN_ROUNDS`), the
   same gate the Verse page uses for this table.
