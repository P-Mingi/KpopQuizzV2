  steps, FAQ, links to the other three languages. `lang` is set before first paint with the inline script pattern of
  `app/(site)/pt/layout.tsx` (in the page component: fr, es, id are not locales of `lib/i18n/config.ts`). Start plays ten
  songs from All K-pop with the v11 engine (`use-run.ts`), in the page language; Play again, Share and the challenge row
  are localized. Static/ISR (`revalidate = 3600`), every read is cookie-free.
- Real numbers. The prototype's "4,120 songs, 79 group playlists" are replaced by `getSongCount()` and the number of

  Until it is applied the call fails and the eyebrow is not rendered. Measured today: no eyebrow on the four landings.

  `isThemedModePlayable`). Breadcrumb, typographic cover, H1, lead, "Play this playlist" (generate's playlist of the

- `/blindtest`. Flag on: the Playlists rail under the hero (link "Play in your language" to `/guess-the-kpop-song`),
  the live band after "Ways to play" (`/live`, `/join`), the language row after the FAQ, the playable themes in the
  playlist menu. A hidden themed mode has no card, no link and no menu item.
- Tracking. Landings need nothing: `btSourceFor()` already maps their paths to `landing-en|fr|es|id` (unit tested) and
