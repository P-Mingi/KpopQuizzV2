### Day 3a · Blog · Hana · draft id 5

**SEVENTEEN's three units, explained** (group: SEVENTEEN)

A quick guide from the KpopQuiz team for newer fans and for anyone preparing for our SEVENTEEN quizzes. Sources are linked below.

SEVENTEEN is a 13 member group under Pledis Entertainment. They debuted with a live showcase on 26 May 2015, and their first EP, 17 Carat, came out on 29 May 2015.

The group is split into three units:
- the hip-hop unit,
- the vocal unit,
- the performance unit.

Each unit releases and performs on its own, and the thirteen come together for the group's title tracks. That structure is why SEVENTEEN questions on this site often ask which unit a member belongs to.

Which unit do you know best?

Sources: [Seventeen (South Korean band), Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seventeen_(South_Korean_band))

### Day 5a · Blog · Remy · draft id 9

**EXO and SHINee: two SM boy group debuts, four years apart** (group: EXO)

A short history from the KpopQuiz team. Sources are linked below.

SHINee came first. Their single "Replay" came out on 22 May 2008, and their first stage was on Inkigayo on 25 May 2008.

EXO followed in 2012 with an idea that was new at the time: two sub-units, EXO-K and EXO-M, debuting together. They released "MAMA" on 8 April 2012, with twelve members at debut.

Both groups came from SM Entertainment, four years apart, and both are in our boy group quizzes and blindtest playlists.

Which debut song do you know better, "Replay" or "MAMA"?

Sources: [SHINee, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shinee) · [EXO, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exo)

### Day 8a · Blog · Mae · draft id 15

**Red Velvet's two sides** (group: Red Velvet)

A short guide from the KpopQuiz team. Sources are linked below.

Red Velvet debuted under SM Entertainment on 1 August 2014, with "Happiness" on Music Bank.

The group's name describes its two sides. As Wikipedia puts it, the "red" side is bright, bold pop, and the "velvet" side is smoother, with R&B and ballads influenced by the 1990s.

That split is useful in a blindtest: if a clip sounds like a bright pop song, think of the red side; if it is slower and smoother, think velvet.

Which side do you play more often?

Sources: [Red Velvet (group), Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Velvet_(group))

**Five girl group facts for your next quiz**

Five facts from the KpopQuiz team, each with its source. Useful before you try our girl group quizzes.
