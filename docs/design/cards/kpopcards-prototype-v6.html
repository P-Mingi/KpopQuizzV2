            How KpopCards works

            A free card game of K-pop idols. Every photo is free to reuse and credited on the card.

            1Open

            A free pack every 10 minutes, up to 10 waiting. The stack fills up again at midnight, Paris time.

            2Collect

            Every idol has up to 6 editions, from Standard to Full Art, and each edition uses its own photo.

            3Complete

            Sets and the weekly list give packs. Extra copies turn into shards, and shards craft the cards you miss.

            No account yet? Open one demo pack. Sign in with your Bunnyverse passport to keep the 5 cards.

            Questions

            Is it free?

            Yes. Packs are never sold, and cards can't be sold or turned into money. The game pays for itself with a few ads on bunnyverse.org.

            Do I need an account?

            Not to try it: anyone can open one demo pack. To keep cards and get a pack every 10 minutes, sign in with your free Bunnyverse passport, the same one as kpopquiz.org.

            Is there crypto, NFTs or a marketplace?

            No. Cards have no money value, there is no market and no auction, and no card is ever numbered to look scarce.

            How do the odds work?

            Each card is drawn on its own with the odds on the Packs page, the same for everyone. The fifth card is Stage or better. Gold guarantee: if 29 packs in a row bring nothing Gold or better, the fifth card of pack 30 is Gold.

            What are shards?

            Extra copies become shards when you recycle them: Standard 1, Stage 2, Holo 5, Era 12, Gold 30, Full Art 80. 50 shards open a pack. You can also craft a card you're missing: Standard 10, Stage 20, Holo 50, Era 120, Gold 300. Full Art only comes from packs.

            Why is a card locked?

            Each edition uses its own free photo, from a different day. An idol with 3 free photos has 3 editions, from Standard up. An idol joins packs with 2 photos, and a new edition opens as soon as another photo is approved.

            What is a half card?

            Group photos are split into two cards, 1/2 and 2/2, so the faces stay big. When you own one half, the same edition gives you the other half.

            Where do the photos come from?

            Only photos under a free licence, mostly from Wikimedia Commons, with everyone in them 18 or older on the day. The author and licence are on every card and on the credits page. Report a photo and it leaves the game within 48 hours.

            Why are there ads?

            Only if you choose: a rewarded ad gives a fixed 25 shards, up to 10 a day. Ads never show in front of a pack, and kpopquiz.org has none.

            Will it send me notifications?

            Only if you turn them on: one when your stack of 10 is full, never between 22:00 and 08:00. The timer gives up to 40 packs a day, so leaving a tab open all day brings nothing more.

            Can I put it on my home screen?

            Yes. On iPhone, tap Share and then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open the browser menu and tap Install app.

            Credits

            Every photo used in the game is listed on the credits page, with its author, licence, source and what was changed (cropped, frame and text added). A card made from a CC BY-SA photo is shared under CC BY-SA too.
