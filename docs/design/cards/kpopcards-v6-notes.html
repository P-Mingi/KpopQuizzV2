  • A bigger card in the middle of the opening: it now takes the room the empty Open buttons used to keep, up to 340 px wide, still clear of the five slots on every screen we tried.

Your note

KpopCards, with its own lobby

  • The top bar says KpopCards, with the rabbit, instead of the Bunnyverse wordmark. The pack and the card back say KpopCards too.
  • The lobby is the home page, like Kyara's: the pack in the centre, then Choose your pack, Can't wait, Almost there, your last pull and six colour boxes.
  • Five tabs: Lobby, Collection, Missions, Ranking, Help. A bell lists what is waiting for you, and your avatar shows your level.
  • Everything links to kpopquiz.org: the passport, Kpopdle and the quiz of the day, each worth a pack.
  • The stack now holds 10 packs, like Kyara (it was 5).

More colour, better structure

  • Each box has its own colour and an icon: violet for your collection, green for this week, the group's own colour for the focus pack, gold for friends, pink for kpopquiz.org, blue for drop rates.
  • Your collection shows a ring with your %, your level, the cards to the next level and your three best cards.

  • Six boxes in three columns, so nothing sits alone.

Your note

The pack no longer covers the slots

  • On a short screen the pack in the opening sat on top of the five empty slots.
  • The pack, the card pile and the final fan now take their size from the screen height, so there is always room for the row of slots under them.

Your note

A smoother opening

  • Nothing behind the pack is drawn while it is open: the lobby is hidden and its timer stops refreshing.
  • Backgrounds fade as layers instead of repainting the whole screen; the light rays are smaller and need no mask.
  • Fewer blend effects on cards and packs, and the Full Art rainbow rim turns as one layer instead of being redrawn every frame.
  • Particles use one canvas pixel per screen pixel, with a cap of 260 at once.
  • The spotlight dims cards with opacity only, and its glow is a layer that fades in.

Your note: more addictive

Reasons to open one more

  • Choose your pack: All idols, Girl groups, Boy groups or Soloists. The pack in the centre takes the colours of your choice.
  • Pack of the day: one group a day, free once, with a countdown to midnight.
  • Golden pack: every 10th pack is golden and its fifth card is Holo or better. A chip counts down: "Golden pack in 2".
  • Open 5: five packs rip together, 25 cards are dealt and turn over, the best one last, with the dark stage if it is Gold or Full Art.
  • Almost there: the idols and groups one or two cards from complete, with a Craft button when you have the shards.
  • Level: one level every 10 cards, with titles from Rookie to Legend. Your level shows on your avatar.
  • Ranking: friends by completion %, with each player's showcase of three cards and earned badges.
  • No streak to lose, no paid packs and no card that disappears forever.

Six research agents · public sources only

The Kyara audit

kyara.games refuses automated visits (it sits behind a bot check called Anubis), and we don't get around bot checks. So six agents read what others publish about it: open-source fan add-ons that work with the site, referral and review pages, scanner reports, Discord listings, YouTube descriptions and posts on X seen through search results. The look and the exact animations could not be seen; send screenshots or a screen recording of its lobby and a pack opening and I will match them closely.

What it is

  • A free web card game of about 6,457 anime and manga characters, made by one person in their spare time, launched on 4 October 2026, in French first. ParrainLink, Gridinsoft
  • Its official Discord lists about 29,000 members; one player claimed 600,000 players by 22 October (unverified). Discord
  • Built with SvelteKit, installable as a web app, behind Cloudflare. Gridinsoft, ScamAdviser
  • Sign-in with Discord only. No licences: it calls itself an unofficial fan project, and critics on X say its images are AI-made.

The loop

  • A free booster every 10 minutes, up to 10 stored, opened 1, 5 or 10 at a time, with a "Tout révéler" (reveal all) button. kyara-manager
  • A themed pack of the day at midnight Paris time, and about 18 themed boosters (Shōnen, Isekai, Waifu and others). kyareeh-plus
  • A golden booster every N boosters, a pity counter (around 250 for a Mythic in a fan copy), featured cards.
  • A "still there?" check that stops people farming boosters with an open tab.

Cards and collection

  • Six rarities by colour: grey, green, blue, purple, gold, and a rainbow border for Mythic. Rarity follows how popular a character is. Better Kyara
  • Albums per series with "?" for missing cards; players built add-ons to show names and completion %. Firefox add-on
  • Each card unlocks a wiki page for its character.
  • Missions for finished series, profile showcase, badges such as Founder and Recruiter, rankings with a friends filter, a notification bell.

Money and risk

  • One currency, chakra, from recycling duplicates and selling. A player market with fixed prices, auctions, buy orders and a 5% tax; gifts capped after abuse. ParrainLink
  • Referral contests with 3 boosters each and a recruiter leaderboard; follow-to-win giveaways on X.
  • Players call it free and fun, and joke about addiction; complaints are about rarity by popularity and AI images. Trustpilot 3.8 from 8 reviews.
  • A culture of card flipping and bots grew around the market.
