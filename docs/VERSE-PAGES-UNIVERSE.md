# VERSE PAGES UNIVERSE - the infinite-depth content model

Companion to VERSE-ROADMAP-V3.md (owner directive 2026-07-30). Three parts:
(1) the real in-depth competitor feature matrix, (2) the exhaustive K-pop
content catalog + the V-PAGES system that makes it buildable, (3) the
canonical space order + structure templates. The governing insight, in the
owner's words: fandoms keep working because it is "a never ending page
entering page entering page"; EVERYTHING can have its own page. A lightstick
can have a page explaining its design, its versions, where to buy it. That
rabbit hole is the retention engine, and we build it better than anyone.

### 2b. V-PAGES - the system that makes ALL of it buildable

The design problem: we cannot schema 40 entity types, and we must not allow
free chaos. The answer sits between: CUSTOM PAGES with KINDS.

- A curator/contributor creates a page inside the space: picks a PAGE KIND
  from a curated kind registry (song-story, mv, choreography, item,
  lightstick, concert, episode, comeback, culture-guide, glossary-entry,
  concept, general). Each kind ships a scaffold: a mini-infobox (typed
  fields appropriate to the kind, sources required on facts) + suggested
  sections + the block editor for prose. Kinds are per-verse CONFIG (the
  V3 architecture law: anime verse later gets arc/character/episode kinds).
- URL: /verse/{group}/wiki/{slug}. FLAT slugs (unique per space), nesting
  expressed by parent page + breadcrumbs, not URL depth (SEO-safer, pages
  can re-parent without link rot).
- THE RABBIT HOLE, mechanically: every internal mention is a hover-preview
  link (extend W3.3 chips to all links); every page ends with its related
  pages (extend the graph footer); typing an @-mention of a page that does
  not exist offers "create this page" (the red-link moment, ours); every
  entity page grows a "More about this" zone listing the custom pages
  attached to it (the album page links its versions page, its MV pages,
  its photocard set).
- QUALITY GATES (how we avoid fandom's junk-drawer fate): publish gate
  stays (draft until it has minimum content); fact-kinds require sources
  per fact field; culture-kinds require none but render a "fan-written"
  badge; min-gate hides empty sections as always; computed quality scores
  custom pages too (stub markers, wanted list); living-persons structural
  exclusions apply to every kind that can reference an idol; banned-terms
  + flags + patrol queue (W4.8) cover all of it; sitemap inclusion only at
  published + non-stub (thin-page SEO protection).
- Attribution: every page carries "started by X, maintained by N fans"
  (credited labor = the moat).
- Migration required (custom_pages + kind registry tables): owner-run,
  standard stop-and-wait.

## PART 3 - ORDER + TEMPLATES

### 3a. The canonical space-home order (the credible first visit)

Design the default as the answer to one question: a curious person just
searched "BTS" and landed here; teach them the group in the order a fan
friend would. LOCKED default order:

1. HERO: name, fandom line, banner, identity moment
2. INTRO: 2-4 sentence "who they are" text block (NEW seo_critical block,
   curator-written, the currently-missing first paragraph)
3. VITALS STRIP: debut year, members count, agency, fandom name + colors
   (one quiet line, not boxes)
4. MEMBERS: faces first; people connect to people before discographies
5. THE STORY SO FAR: short folded overview prose (V-TEXT folding)
6. LATEST + LANDMARK RELEASES: what to listen to first
7. NOW: current era / countdown / live strip (contextual, hides when idle)
8. GO DEEPER: the explore grid into the wiki (eras, awards, tours, culture
   pages, glossary: the rabbit-hole doorway, powered by V-PAGES)
9. COLLECTIONS teaser
10. COMMUNITY: activity, discussions, join
11. PLAY: the games module (one tasteful widget/link, per V-SPACE-FLOW)
12. Related-graph footer (fixed)

Curators can still reorder (W-CUSTOM freedom stands) but THIS is the default
every template starts from, and the Minimalist template keeps exactly this.

### 3b. Structure templates at space creation (structural, not visual)

Orthogonal to the 6 visual presets (look) - these decide STRUCTURE (tabs,
modules, page scaffolds). Offered at creation, switchable later:

1. STARTER (recommended, say so in the UI): 4 tabs (Home · Members · Music ·
   Community). Home = the canonical order, items 1-6 + 10 + 12. No empty
   doorways: era/wiki/collections appear when content exists.
2. COMPLETE: 6 tabs (+ Story, + Collections). Adds eras/timeline scaffolds
   as DRAFTS for the curator to fill (drafts, so no ghost pages), the
   explore grid, the interactive trackers.
3. ENCYCLOPEDIA: 7 tabs (+ Wiki index). Everything on: awards, tours,
   appearances, glossary, fanchants, culture-page scaffolds, full V-PAGES
   kind set enabled. For big established fandoms with a curator team.
4. EMPTY: the bare seo_critical skeleton only. For curators who want to
   build every choice themselves. (UI notes Starter is the recommended
   beginning; Empty is respected, not pushed.)

Templates are config bundles (tab set + module set + which kinds are enabled
+ draft scaffolds), pure data on the W-CUSTOM system: no new rendering tech.

## PART 4 - THE PALWORLD DEEP TEARDOWN (16 pages explored, 2026-07-30)

Full structural exploration of palworld.wiki.fextralife.com: every distinct
page template (index, leaf, calculator, map, guide, tier list, changelog,
lore archive, concept matrix, hub, FAQ, encounter index, data tables). The
2k+ pages are all instances of ~5 template types over one canonical dataset.

### 4a. THE structural lesson (governs V-PAGES)

Their wiki is not a fandom-style wiki; it is a GENERATED DATABASE with
editorial pages on top: one mined dataset -> templated page types -> tools
as views over the same data. Every number is cross-consistent (the "288" in
the page title = the filter counter = the card count). Counts + provenance
are the trust currency. This is EXACTLY our architecture (typed entities ->
templated pages -> tools), so we adopt their patterns with confidence: we
already have the hard part (the canonical dataset).

### 4b. Adopt list (with the K-pop translation)

INDEX MACHINERY
- Entity mega-index with facet filters + live counts ("288 matching · 288
  total") + search-within-list + Reset. K-pop: the Idol Deck (filter by
  company, generation, position, status), discography filters (year, type).
- Jump-anchor chip rows with counts ("Dark Pals 54"). K-pop: "4th Gen 38 ·
  5th Gen 21", "Title tracks 24 · B-sides 190".
- Card microformat: image, name, ID, attribute chips, capability line, one
  HOOK line ("Partner: Fluffy Shield"). K-pop: idol card hook ("Signature:
  the 'Fever' killing part").
- Index-of-indexes router pages: category cards with counts + one-liners.
- Counts EVERYWHERE incl. titles/og ("All 288 Pals"). K-pop: "All 214
  SEVENTEEN songs" - completeness claims are the SEO play. Cross-promo
  cards show page counts ("/verse/army · 2,300 pages") as social proof.

LEAF PAGES
- Anatomy: infobox + description + stats + how-obtained + related tables +
  curated onward exits. K-pop song page: credits infobox, chart stats
  (sourced), release context, versions.
- Quality-tier tables (Common->Legendary stat rows). K-pop: album VERSION
  MATRIX (one row per version: tracklist deltas, photocard counts).
- Comparative stat framing ("bars relative to the highest any Pal reaches").
  K-pop: "most B-sides of any 4th-gen group", computed from our data.

TOOLS (the calculator doctrine)
- THE PATTERN: interactive tool layered over a FULLY PUBLISHED static truth
  table (SEO-visible, verifiable, works without JS). Their breeding
  calculator sits above the complete combo table. Ours must do the same.
- K-pop tool candidates: lineup/unit resolver (pick two members -> every
  subunit/duet/co-appearance they share), photocard pull explorer (album
  version -> possible card pool), discography completion tracker.
- Interactive map with layers + multi-compare + toggles. K-pop: world tour
  map (plot artists' tour stops, compare up to 6, filter by era/year, pins
  link to concert pages).
- Tools live at NAV level, not buried.

EDITORIAL PATTERNS
- Tier/ranked pages WITH a methodology disclosure block ("Combat 50%, Work
  30%, Mobility 20%; S = top 5%"). K-pop: data-backed rankings with "how
  this ranking works" - the block that separates data product from Twitter
  bait. Plus a "Hot picks" cross-axis strip ("Gateway songs").
- Top-N leaderboard + overflow escape hatch ("32 more have Kindling - see
  the filter") - every teaser links the filtered index AND a deep page.
- Concept matrix pages (Elements type chart). K-pop: concept/era matrix
  (cute/dark/retro x groups), each cell a count-link.
- Progression-sorted "start here" ladders. K-pop: new-fan discography
  ladder ("work down it as your fandom gets stronger").
- Full-text primary-source archives with per-source counts (their 64 lore
  notes). K-pop: universe/lore note archives (BU-style storylines),
  interview archives where legally quotable (fan-written summaries + links
  otherwise).
- Changelog table: ISO date + link OUT to canonical source, with a
  "Current" callout. K-pop: comeback/news timeline citing official notices.
- FAQ with bolded numbers + proof links into our own pages.
- "How to use this page" bolded box under the H1 of every complex surface.
- Provenance lines + last-edited stamps + revision permalinks on every
  page (we have the machinery; surface it as the standard trust footer).

### 4c. Do-NOT-copy list

- Duplicated chrome (nav rendered twice, double breadcrumbs) - render once.
- 100+ link alphabetical category walls on leaf pages - curated related
  grid (6-8) + one "view full index" link instead.
- Raw data-mined IDs shipped publicly (their "Blueprint_Head003_1" rows) -
  our publish gate already forbids this; stay firm.
- Repeated near-identical tables (8 shop pools) - one table + facet filter.
- Infobox rendered twice per page - one responsive component.
- Giant raw select dropdowns (500 options) - typeahead combobox with
  images, always.
- Off-site discussion links - our per-page discussions are native; keep it
  that way.
- Restrictive custom license buried in footer - our charter does the
  opposite, loudly.
- Invisible contributors (no credit anywhere) - our credited-curator model
  is the direct counter; their weakness is our moat.

### 4d. Personalization tie-in (the owner's law: no fandom looks alike)

Every adopted pattern above enters the W-CUSTOM BLOCK REGISTRY, not a fixed
layout: index pages, tools, tier pages, matrices, archives are page KINDS
and MODULES that curators enable, arrange, theme and frame per space. One
space leads with the tour map, another with the lore archive, another with
the photocard explorer. The skeleton (SEO, counts, provenance, trust
footer) is identical; the arrangement, selection and skin never are. The
structure templates (3b) preselect sensible bundles; the studio does the
rest.
